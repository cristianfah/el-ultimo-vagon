# MiniMax H3: formato de prompt

Resumen de la guía oficial (`h3-prompt-writing`, en [MiniMax-AI/MiniMax-H3](https://github.com/MiniMax-AI/MiniMax-H3), en `skills/h3-prompt-writing/references/`). Si hay dudas, manda la guía oficial. `prompt_expansion_mode: disabled` siempre: el prompt ya va en el formato del modelo.

## Qué modo usar

| Nuestra ruta en fal | Modo oficial | Cuándo |
|---|---|---|
| `image-to-video` con `image_url` | **I2VA** | Plano suelto desde su keyframe (estrategia B) |
| `image-to-video` con `image_url` + `end_image_url` | **FL2VA** | Solo con las condiciones de FL (`agentes/asistente_direccion.md`) |
| `reference-to-video` | **Ref2VA** | Bloque multi-beat con hojas, placas y keyframes (estrategia A). Es el modo por defecto |

## I2VA (un plano desde su keyframe)

```text
For the target video, at 0.00 seconds into the target video, <Picture 1> (from [Shot 1]) is fully referenced.

integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Live-action, cinematic, [shot size and framing limits]. [First-frame anchor: who is where, facing what, looking at what; what is behind them and whether it moves]. [Wardrobe and state anchors that must hold]. [Camera: type + amplitude + speed, own sentence, with its motive]. [Beat by beat, with seconds: states, not transitions; gaze targets; hands and side of frame]. [Speaker description] (S1) [delivery] <d>[Spanish] line exactly as scripted</d> [what the body does after the line]. [Light kept steady; any change with its source]. [Local positive locks]. No music. No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text.

overall_soundscape: [1–4 sentences: continuous ambience, physical sounds with their moment, non-verbal human sounds].

non_diegetic_music: N/A
```

Orden recomendado por la guía: **anclaje en el primer fotograma → inicio de la acción → desarrollo → resultado o reacción.**

## FL2VA (primer y último fotograma)

```text
How the reference pictures align with the target video — Picture 1 (from Shot 1) aligns with the 0.00-second mark of the target video; Picture 2 (from Shot 1) aligns with the S.SS-second mark of the target video.

integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Live-action, cinematic, [begins in the position and framing of Picture 1]. [The path: observable intermediate changes, second by second, with what stays identical]. [Settles into the exact state of Picture 2 at the end].

overall_soundscape: ...

non_diegetic_music: N/A
```

- Un solo plano. No se describen las dos imágenes: se describe **el camino** entre ellas.
- `S.SS` es la duración con dos decimales.

## Ref2VA (bloque con referencias, multi-beat)

Seis secciones, en este orden. `detailed_description` va de **350 a 500 palabras**, repartidas según la información de cada plano.

```text
subject_definitions:
<Subject 1> is Martina, from <Picture 1> (character sheet): [critical visible anchors for this block].
<Subject 2> is Tomás, from <Picture 2>: [...].
<Subject 3> is the last carriage of the night train, from <Picture 3>: [geography: aisle, seat rows, door with its small window, windows on each side, light sources].
<Picture 4> is the first frame of [Shot 1], showing [...].
<Picture 5> is the keyframe of [Shot 2], showing [...].

summary:
[keyframe completion + reference generation] [One short paragraph: what the block shows and the role of each reference. No plot summary.]

retention_analysis:
<Subject 1> (appears in [Shot 1], [Shot 2]): fully_preserved - [face, hair, cream sweater...].
<Subject 3> (appears in [Shot 1]–[Shot 5]): fully_preserved - [layout, amber lamps, rain on the side windows...].
<Picture 4> ([Shot 1] first frame): fully_preserved - [...].

detailed_description:
The target video is live-action, cinematic, [look in one or two sentences from direccion_arte].
[Shot 1] [Framing and first frame; location map and background; each subject with position, orientation and gaze; camera with type + amplitude + speed and motive; acting beats with seconds; dialogue with (Sx) and <d>; sounds of the moment; local locks.]
[Shot 2] At 00:03.500, the camera cuts to [...].
[...]

overall_soundscape:
[...]

non_diegetic_music:
N/A
```

Etiquetas: `<Subject N>` es contenido reutilizable (persona, locación, prop); `<Picture N>` es un fotograma concreto (primer fotograma o keyframe de un plano). Una imagen que solo define a un personaje no lleva línea propia de `<Picture N>`: se cita dentro de su `<Subject N>`.

## Reglas comunes

- **Planos:** `[Shot 1]` sin tiempo; después, `[Shot N] At MM:SS.mmm, the camera cuts to…`, con tiempos crecientes. Si solo cambia la distancia o un poco el ángulo, no es un corte: es un movimiento.
- **Cámara:** tipo + amplitud + velocidad, escrito como acción:
  - *The camera pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed toward her eyes.*
  - *The camera trucks left at slow speed along the aisle, passing the seat backs in the foreground.*
  - *The camera shakes slightly, as if handheld by someone standing in the aisle, and arrives late to the knock.*
  - *The camera holds a static shot as she reads the message.* (Solo con el motivo escrito por el Director de fotografía.)

  Vocabulario: *Push In / Pull Out, Zoom In / Zoom Out, Pan Left / Right, Truck Left / Right, Tilt Up / Down, Pedestal Up / Down, Arc Shot, Tracking Shot, Static Shot, Shake Slightly / Strongly, POV, Roll*. Amplitud: *with small / large amplitude*. Velocidad: *at slow / fast speed*.
- **Diálogo:** cada voz tiene un id estable, `(S1)`, `(S2)`, para todo el bloque. La primera vez se describe quién es y cómo suena (edad, timbre, ritmo, acento). Dentro de `<d>` van solo la etiqueta de idioma y la línea exacta: `<d>[Spanish] Sí.</d>`. Si alguien no habla, no lleva id.
- **Voz en off:** «says in an off-screen voiceover», y justo después, que los labios del personaje en cuadro quedan cerrados.
- **Texto en cuadro:** solo el que existe en el mundo (la pantalla del celular), entre comillas dobles: *the lock screen reads "23:38" and "Estoy en el tren."*
- **`overall_soundscape`:** ambiente, sonidos físicos y sonidos humanos no verbales. El diálogo no se repite aquí.
- **`non_diegetic_music`:** siempre `N/A` (la música va en post con Suno).
- **Cierre fijo:** en todos los modos, la descripción principal (`integrated_multimodal_description` o `detailed_description`) termina con «No music. No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text.».
- **Duración:** H3 genera clips de 5 a 15 s. En un plano suelto de 1–2 s, la acción ocurre en los primeros segundos y después hay un estado quieto pero vivo (respiración, ojos) para que el montaje corte.
